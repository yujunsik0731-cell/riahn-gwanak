From layered cuts
to men's dandy cuts

Every cut at RIAHN Hair Gwanak Hyundai starts with a 1:1 consultation with your designer. Director Mira focuses on layered cuts for short bobs, medium and long hair; designer Taeri focuses on men's dandy cuts and design cuts.

Senior Team Leader Doa specializes in men's iron perms and down perms. Want waves or volume too? See our perms & straightening guide.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a layered cut — short bob, medium or long
  • Men looking for a dandy cut or men's design cut
  • Those who want a down perm to keep the sides neat
  • Anyone near Bongcheon or Seoul Nat'l Univ. Station who wants to choose their designer

Haircut FAQ

Do haircut prices differ by designer?
Yes. Haircuts are ₩22,000–₩35,000 depending on designer level. Blow-dry is ₩20,000–₩35,000.
Do you have a men's hair specialist?
Designer Taeri specializes in men's dandy cuts, parted perms and design cuts, and Senior Team Leader Doa specializes in men's straightening, iron perms and down perms.
How much is a down perm?
A down perm is ₩40,000–₩55,000 depending on designer level.
