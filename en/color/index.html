FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

How much is a root touch-up?
A Shiseido root touch-up is ₩65,000 and a Milbon root touch-up is ₩55,000.
Are there separate prices for men's color?
Yes. Milbon Color (Men) S ₩90,000 · M ₩120,000, Shiseido Color (Men) S ₩110,000 · M ₩140,000, and Shiseido Color × Shiseido Detox Care (Men) S ₩180,000 · M ₩210,000.
How do Shiseido and Milbon color prices compare?
At S length: Milbon Color ₩90,000, Shiseido Color ₩120,000, and Shiseido Color × Shiseido Repair Care ₩220,000. Longer hair costs extra.
