Hair care and scalp care
in one place

We offer head spa and scalp care. Hair care menus include the Natura Hair Treatment (Cinderella), Shiseido Premium Taimetic Treatment and Shiseido Repair Care, alongside AYUNCHE scalp care and ampoules.

Treatment & Scalp Care FAQ

How much is scalp care?
AYUNCHE Scalp Care is ₩50,000. We offer head spa and scalp care at the salon.
Can I get a treatment together with a perm or color?
Yes. Our perm, straightening and color menus include combinations with Shiseido Repair Care (Shiseido Detox Care for men). Shiseido Repair Care on its own is ₩140,000.
How much are the ampoule treatments?
The AYUNCHE All-in-One Ampoule is ₩50,000 and the AYUNCHE Pro Bonding Ampoule is ₩30,000.
