About

Every visit starts with
a 1:1 consultation

RIAHN Hair Gwanak Hyundai is at the Bongcheon Hyundai Market intersection in Bongcheon-dong, Gwanak-gu, Seoul (2F WS Tower, 110 Euncheon-ro), between Bongcheon and Seoul Nat'l Univ. stations on subway Line 2.

Open since 2014, our six designers — three of them RIAHN Stylist Award winners — offer straightening for curly and coarse hair, volume straightening, layered cuts and perms, and men's dandy cuts and parted perms, each after a 1:1 consultation.

Free parking is available in the building's basement lot and parking tower, and pets are welcome. Open Tuesday to Sunday from 10:30 (closed Mondays).

Good to know

  • Local currency vouchers, Seoul Pay, Zero Pay and prepaid cards accepted
  • Earn points with Naver Pay (up to 7%, subject to Naver's policy)
  • Head spa and scalp care available
  • Pets welcome
  • Separate men's and women's restrooms; wheelchair-accessible entrance and restroom; accessible parking
  • Free parking in the basement lot and parking tower (busy on weekends)
