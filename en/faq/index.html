FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about booking, parking, opening hours, prices and payment.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book a date and time on Naver Booking. You can also call us (+82-2-878-7590) or send an Instagram DM (@riahn_gwanak_official).
Where is the salon?
2F WS Tower, 110 Euncheon-ro, Gwanak-gu, Seoul (서울 관악구 은천로 110 더블유에스타워 2층), in the Shinhan Bank building at the Bongcheon Hyundai Market intersection — between Bongcheon and Seoul Nat'l Univ. stations on Line 2.
Is there parking?
Yes — free parking in the basement lot and parking tower behind the building. It can be busy on weekends.
What are your opening hours?
Tuesday to Friday 10:30–21:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:30–20:00. Closed every Monday. Available booking times may vary with each designer's schedule.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩22,000–₩35,000, perms and straightening ₩25,000–₩370,000, color ₩55,000–₩290,000, treatments and scalp care ₩30,000–₩250,000. Haircut prices vary by designer level, and longer hair costs extra.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Local currency vouchers, Seoul Pay, Zero Pay and prepaid cards are accepted. Paying with Naver Pay earns points (up to 7%, subject to Naver's policy).
What happens on my first visit?
Your designer starts with a 1:1 consultation before the service. You can find the right designer for your service on our Designers page.
Can I request a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when booking. Owner Director Sunny takes bookings from existing clients.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes, pets are welcome.
Is the salon wheelchair accessible?
The entrance and restroom are wheelchair accessible, and there are accessible parking spaces. Men's and women's restrooms are separate.

Still have questions? Call us during opening hours (Tue–Fri 10:30–21:00, Sat–Sun 10:30–20:00, closed Mondays).
